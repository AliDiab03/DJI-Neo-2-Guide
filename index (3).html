Questions people ask first

Short answers before you install.

Is this an official DJI app?

No. This is an independent, unofficial guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by DJI. Product names and trademarks belong to their owners and are used only to identify the drone the guide is about.

Can I fly my drone with this app?

No. The app is a guide and does not control the drone. To fly, use the official app from the manufacturer. This guide helps you set up, prepare and troubleshoot.

Does it need internet?

The guides and PDF manual work offline. An internet connection is only used to show ads and to open outside links such as the manufacturer's support page.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

What data does the app collect?

The app has no accounts and does not ask for personal details. Read the full privacy policy for what the ad provider may process.
