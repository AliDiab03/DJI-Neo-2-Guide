Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the Neo 2 Drone Guide app ("the app", "we", "us") handles, how advertising works in the app, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process your device's advertising ID and similar technical data to serve and measure ads.
  • Where required by law, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.
  • Your checklist progress stays on your device only.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly. We do not run our own servers for the app and we do not create user profiles.

Information stored on your device

The app saves small settings, such as which pre-flight checklist items you have ticked, in the app's private storage on your device. This information is not sent to us. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. When ads are requested, Google and its partners may automatically collect and process the following, depending on your consent and device settings:

3. Consent and your ad choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and some US states. Ads are requested only when your choices allow it.

You can limit or turn off ad personalization at any time:

  • On your device: open Android Settings and look for Google, then Ads (the exact path varies by device) to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • On the web: manage your preferences at adssettings.google.com.
  • In the app: if a privacy options entry is offered, use it to review your consent choices.

If you opt out of personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

4. Permissions

The app requests only two Android permissions:

  • Internet to load ads and open links you tap.
  • Network state to check whether a connection is available.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or precise location.

The app contains links to outside sites and apps, such as the manufacturer's website and support pages, Google Play, and search results. We do not control those services and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies before using them.

6. Children's privacy

The app is a general audience guide and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data retention and security

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Locally stored settings remain on your device until you clear them. Data processed by Google is kept under Google's own policies.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, most requests about ad-related data are handled through Google using the controls described in section 3. You can also contact us at your@email.com and we will help point you in the right direction.
